Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Who we are

LaTimp is an app for due dates, payments and free time. The data controller is the operator of the LaTimp app [to be completed], registered at [to be completed]. For any question about your data, write to [to be completed].

What data we use

  • Account: the email address you sign in with. The account is optional; without it everything stays on your phone.
  • Agenda: due dates, amounts, notes, people and gifts, lists, settings. You enter them and we use them only to show your agenda and send your reminders.
  • Phone reminders: if you turn them on, we keep the technical address your phone uses to receive notifications and your reminder schedule (the item's title, date and time).
  • Family: the dates you choose to share are encrypted on your phone with the family code before they leave it. The server only receives encrypted text and a fingerprint of the code; we cannot read them.
  • Votes with friends: the ideas put to the vote, the name you type when voting and your choice.
  • Reports: the text you send, the screenshot if you attach one, and technical details (app version, screen size, device type).
  • Errors: the technical error message, the version and device type, without your agenda. Email addresses in the message are removed before sending.
  • Usage statistics: only if you turn them on in More. They are numbers (for example how many dates you have and on how many days you used the app), without your agenda.
  • Technical connection data: your IP address and browser type reach the providers that deliver the app, for operation and security.

Where it is kept

  • On your phone, in the app's storage.
  • If you have an account: at Supabase, in the Frankfurt data centre (Germany, European Union): the database, attached files and server functions.
  • The app's code is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Who else receives it

  • Supabase (database, accounts, files, server functions), in the EU.
  • GitHub (app hosting), jsDelivr (the server connection library) and Google Fonts (the app's font) receive your IP address when the app loads.
  • Google delivers the sign-in emails (code or link).
  • Your phone's notification service (Google for Android and Chrome, Apple for iPhone, Mozilla for Firefox) delivers the reminders, encrypted.
  • Some of these providers are outside the EU (United States). Transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.
  • We don't sell data and don't use it for advertising. If we later use an artificial intelligence service for ideas or photo reading, we will ask for your consent first, and the text or photo will be sent only when you ask.

Why we use it (legal basis)

  • To provide the service you asked for: the agenda, the account, reminders, family, votes (performance of the contract).
  • With your consent: usage statistics and, in the future, the artificial intelligence features. You can withdraw it at any time in More.
  • For security, abuse prevention and fixing errors (legitimate interest).

How long we keep it

  • Account data, as long as you have an account. “Delete account” in More removes from the server your account, agenda, reminders, notification subscriptions, family, votes and files.
  • Sent reminders are deleted after at most 30 days.
  • Statistics are deleted when you turn them off.
  • Reports and errors are kept as long as needed to fix the problem.
  • “Delete all data” removes what is on the phone.

Your rights

  • Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. For portability use “Backup to a file” in More.
  • Withdrawing consent at any time, without affecting what was done before.
  • For any request write to [to be completed]. We answer within one month.
  • You can complain to the Romanian data protection authority (ANSPDCP), www.dataprotection.ro.

Storage on your device

The app keeps data in the browser's or phone's storage (for example your agenda and sign-in session). We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Security

The connection to the server is encrypted. Each account sees only its own data. Family data is encrypted on the phone. The app PIN stays only on your phone.

Children

LaTimp is not meant for children under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will announce it in the app. Last updated: 27 November 2026.
